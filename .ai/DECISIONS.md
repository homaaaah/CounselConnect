| ADR-019 | Authentication (approved 2026-09-05, formerly pending ADR-P01): Students sign in with student number; staff sign in with email. | ADR-025 | Expression-cue library (approved 2026-09-10, implemented 2026-10-26; resolves ADR-P06): `@vladmandic/face-api` (maintained face-api API) using the package's local WebGL/WASM backend, with TinyFaceDetector + FaceExpressionNet weights served from the application origin and lazy-loaded at the first allowed session. Seven basic-expression label set; about ten frames are averaged and a result is shown only when at least three samples exist and the top averaged label reaches 60%; below that, send `unavailable` and show nothing rather than a low-confidence guess. | ADR-028 | Scheduled online counseling (approved 2026-09-16): confirmation does not create a conversation. The appointment-derived launcher opens a lobby 30 minutes before start; a participant's first join at or after start (the scheduled session page submits this authorized join request automatically under ADR-038; the request remains required) lazily creates the single `APPOINTMENT` conversation. REST is the durable source for messages/history and native WebSockets deliver committed events. The safety deadline is slot end plus 15 minutes; timeout closes chat with reason `TIMEOUT` but leaves the appointment `CONFIRMED` for the Counselor to record `COMPLETED` or `NO_SHOW`. Students may cancel/reschedule only until 24 hours before start; the assigned Counselor may change mode in place only before start and before chat activity. Closed history is Counselor-only until the 30-day body purge. Reminders use in-app/browser delivery only and contain no counseling content. |

| ADR-029 | Automated COR screening and self-confirmation (approved 2026-09-30; supersedes ADR-003's Guidance Staff COR-review duty and ADR-024's human review): the current COR remains the only enrollment evidence. On upload the backend screens the PDF in-request (format-marker score + OCR field extraction + embedded-barcode decode via configurable `pdftotext`/`pdftoppm`/`tesseract` and `zxing-cpp`). A screening becomes `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` only when the format score and extraction confidence meet configured thresholds AND the barcode decodes and matches the extracted student number; otherwise it is `NEEDS_RESUBMISSION` for the Student to re-upload. The Student confirms the extracted academic fields (strict UCC student-number pattern `^\\d{8}-[A-Za-z]$`; campus/program must match existing active reference rows, never auto-created), which atomically activates the account with `valid_until` (12 months). There is no human approval queue; Guidance Staff retains no COR duty pending a future decision. Verified academic fields (student number, names, year level, section, academic year, and any campus/program the COR mapped to) are read-only during confirmation: the Student confirms or rejects, and a reject sets `NEEDS_RESUBMISSION`/`REJECTED_BY_STUDENT` for a re-upload; the backend also rejects any request that edits a verified field. The barcode's academic-period value is cross-checked against OCR, the decode result/confidence is recorded, and the enrollment-validity date printed on the COR is used for `valid_until` when still in the future (otherwise a 12-month default). Privacy is unchanged: COR bytes stay in the private store (10 MB, seven-day TTL) and are deleted after confirmation/resubmission; raw OCR text and raw barcode payloads are never persisted or logged (payload SHA-256 digest only); missing screening tooling fails closed (no activation). Residual risk: document self-consistency does not prove University issuance, so auto-activation is spoofable until an authoritative registrar source exists. Implementation: `cor_screening` module + migration `20260930_automated_cor_screening`; config in `app.config`. |

| ADR-030 | Process-flow fidelity (approved 2026-09-30): (7) Students may sign in with their registered **email or student number**; staff (Counselor, Guidance Staff, Superadmin) sign in with email. (8) A decoder/processing failure that cannot be attributed to the document is recorded as **`FAILED`** with a safe code (`TECHNICAL_ERROR`) and offers retry/support; unreadable/low-confidence/barcode-inconsistency remains `NEEDS_RESUBMISSION`. (9) The screening record enters **`PROCESSING`** (committed) before it is resolved, so the stage is observable. (11) A **`SUPERADMIN`** role is added (migration `20260930_superadmin_role` widens `chk_users_role`): it handles account recovery and operational exceptions, is read-only on the student directory, may reset a non-active Student to `PENDING_VERIFICATION` (`POST /accounts/students/{user_id}/recover`, audit `account_recovery`), and must not declare a COR authentic or approve registration. |

| ADR-031 | In-modal COR verification without auto-login (approved 2026-10-01): registration no longer signs the Student in. `POST /accounts/register/student-with-cor` and `POST /cor-screenings/resubmit` return a one-time `verification_token` (256-bit random, returned once, never in URLs); the client sends it in the **`X-COR-Token`** header to authorize `POST /cor-screenings/confirm\|reject\|resubmit` for that screening only. The token is stored only as a SHA-256 digest (`cor_screenings.verification_token_hash BINARY(32)` plus `verification_token_issued_at`; migration `20261001_cor_verification_token`), expires with the existing seven-day evidence window (no new TTL setting), is rotated on re-upload, and is destroyed on activation (`PASSED`) or TTL failure (`FAILED`). Missing/expired/tampered/rotated-away tokens fail closed with `401 INVALID_VERIFICATION_TOKEN` (generic message; the raw token is never logged) and token-bearing responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`. A present-but-invalid token never falls back to session auth; token requests are cookie-less, so CSRF does not apply. The signed-in `#registration` session path is retained as the fallback when the modal token is lost (closed tab). |

| ADR-032 | Student profile-edit requests (approved 2026-10-01): during verification a Student may request edits to **names, `year_level`, and `section`** — never `student_number`, `academic_period`, or a mapped `campus`/`program`. `POST /cor-screenings/request-edit` (Student session or `X-COR-Token`) activates the account with the **COR-verified** values and, only when an editable value differs, stores one `PENDING` `profile_change_requests` row (migration `20261002_profile_change_requests`; one pending per Student; a new screening auto-rejects any pending request as `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_COR`). An **active Superadmin** reviews `GET /profile-change-requests` and decides `POST /profile-change-requests/{change_request_id}/approve\|reject` (reject reason required); approving applies the requested names/year_level/section to the profile. This is a narrow carve-out of ADR-030: the Superadmin ratifies a Student-initiated correction, **not** a COR or a registration, and still must not declare a COR authentic. No Student notification in v1. |

| ADR-033 | Registration failure isolation + Student self-cancellation (approved 2026-10-01): a **technical** screening failure (`derive_outcome == FAILED` / `TECHNICAL_ERROR`) creates **no account** — `POST /accounts/register/student-with-cor` deletes the uploaded COR and returns `503 SCREENING_FAILED`, so nothing is persisted and the email/student number stay free; `NEEDS_RESUBMISSION` still creates the `PENDING_VERIFICATION` account. During verification **Reject account** is offered on the review and re-upload steps (`POST /cor-screenings/reject-account`, Student session or `X-COR-Token`, `204`) and **Re-upload COR** is offered only when the screening needs resubmission (it opens the upload form; `POST /cor-screenings/resubmit`); Reject account deletes the account, its screenings/files, sessions, profile, and pending profile-edit requests so the email and student number become reusable, recorded once as `account_rejected`. Self-cancellation is limited to `PENDING_VERIFICATION` accounts (never a previously active account, which may own counseling history); the legacy `POST /cor-screenings/reject` is retired from the UI (kept for compatibility). |

| ADR-034 | Appointment-bound WebRTC calls (approved 2026-10-23): the owning Student and assigned Counselor of a `CONFIRMED` `ONLINE` appointment may join a one-to-one call from the scheduled session page from the scheduled start until appointment end plus the existing 15-minute grace. Signaling uses an authenticated appointment WebSocket (`GET /appointments/{appointment_id}/call` for window/ICE config; `WS /appointments/{appointment_id}/call/ws` for SDP/ICE relay); the server reauthorizes participant/status/mode/time on connect and every 30 seconds without renewing idle activity. Media is peer-to-peer; the server never records or persists media, SDP, ICE candidates, IP addresses, call history, or call audit events, and a call never creates/joins chat or changes the appointment outcome. ICE servers are environment-configured (`COUNSELCONNECT_CALL_ICE_SERVERS`, public STUN default); TURN static credentials are the v1 compromise and must be replaced by ephemeral credentials later. One live connection per participant; the second participant to become ready initiates the offer; unknown/oversized signals and unauthorized connections close with `1008`. Manual `#video-demo` signaling is retired. Implementation: `backend/app/modules/calls`, `frontend/src/features/calls`; tests in `test_calls.py` and `appointment-call.test.cjs`. |

| ADR-035 | Counseling session records and private session files (approved 2026-10-25): each appointment has one `session_records` row containing a Counselor-only autosaved `notepad` plus `presenting_concern`, `session_summary`, `intervention`, `plan`, and optional `follow_up_on`, with `DRAFT`/`FINAL` (`DRAFT`/`FINAL` simplifies the earlier five-state plan; scheduling state stays on the appointment). Finalizing requires the four assessment fields, locks the row, and is audited; reopening is limited to a finalized record on a still-`CONFIRMED` appointment (**widened to `CONFIRMED`/`COMPLETED` by ADR-037**). The assigned Counselor reads everything; the Student reads only the finalized assessment (never the notepad) and only after the appointment is marked `COMPLETED` (drafts, `CONFIRMED`, and other outcomes return 404; files stay available independently); Superadmin gets no content. `session_documents` stores appointment-scoped PDF/JPEG/PNG metadata with random private storage keys and SHA-256; the assigned Student and Counselor upload/download, uploader or Counselor soft-deletes, bytes never enter MySQL, and downloads are `no-store` + attachment. Students may use file endpoints only after the appointment is `COMPLETED` (`409 DOCUMENT_ACCESS_CLOSED`); the Counselor keeps access while `CONFIRMED` or `COMPLETED`. Validation: extension + magic bytes + Pillow decode + PDF EOF, 10 MB cap. Uploads only for `CONFIRMED`/`COMPLETED`. No automatic purge (ADR-P12) and no encryption at rest (ADR-P11) yet. Correction requests/amendments remain deferred. Implementation: migration `20261025_session_records`, appointments module endpoints, `SessionRecordWorkspace` + `SessionRecordPage`; tests `test_session_records.py` and `session-records.test.cjs`. |

| ADR-037 | Session-page mark-as-done and reopen-after-completion (approved 2026-10-25): the scheduled session page replaces **Finalize record** with **Mark as done**. `POST /appointments/{appointment_id}/mark-done` (assigned Counselor, no body) locks the appointment, requires `CONFIRMED` and a reached scheduled start, then in one transaction finalizes a saved `DRAFT` record (all four assessment fields required; otherwise `409 RECORD_INCOMPLETE` with `details.fields`), accepts an already-`FINAL` record without duplicating the finalize audit, releases the slot, closes the linked conversation with `COUNSELOR_OUTCOME`, sets `COMPLETED`, and audits `appointment_completed`. Missing record -> `409 RECORD_NOT_SAVED`; pre-start -> `409 SESSION_NOT_STARTED`. Counselor History `/complete` and `/no-show` remain status-only and never finalize; the standalone record page keeps `/record/finalize`. A finalized record may be reopened while the appointment is `CONFIRMED` or `COMPLETED` (widening ADR-035); the reopened `DRAFT` stays invisible to the Student (`404`) until re-finalized, and session files follow appointment status only, so reopening does not re-lock them. No schema change. Implementation: appointments service/router, `useAppointmentChat.markDone`, `SessionRecordWorkspace` rail; tests `test_session_records.py` (service + HTTP), `scheduled-chat.test.cjs`, `session-records.test.cjs`. **ADR-038 moves the control from the Record panel to the session header; the endpoint, save-first sequence, and validation are unchanged.** ADR-036 is reserved for the separate video-call permission decision. |

| ADR-038 | Session auto-join, scrollable panels, and header Mark as done (approved 2026-10-25): when the scheduled session page is open at/after `messaging_opens_at`, it submits the existing `POST /appointments/{appointment_id}/session/join` once for the current role if that participant has not joined (`student_joined_at`/`counselor_joined_at` is null) and `can_join` is true. The request is still required and authorized server-side (participant, `CONFIRMED` `ONLINE`, join window); the server lazily creates/reuses the conversation and records the join. `can_join` stays true after joining, so the participant timestamp is the already-joined signal, and a one-shot client ref prevents a failed attempt from retrying every tick; a failure shows the error plus a **Retry joining** control. The automatic attempt is a background request that does not renew idle activity; the manual retry is a normal user action. The session page and the standalone `#session-record/{id}` page scroll their tab content inside the panel (desktop `100dvh` viewport layout; bounded mobile panels) without changing data, navigation, or permissions. The Counselor's **Mark as done** control moves from the Record panel to the session header beside **Mark no-show**; it still saves the current assessment first (no PUT for an already-`FINAL` record), calls the unchanged `/mark-done` endpoint, and is shown only after a successful record load. Removing the panel control does not change the endpoint, validation, or atomic finalize+complete step; the standalone record page keeps **Finalize record** and **Reopen for editing**. No API, schema, permission, or Student file-visibility change. Implementation: `ScheduledSessionPage`, `SessionRecordPage`, `SessionRecordWorkspace`, `useSessionRecord`/`useSessionRecordEditor`, `useAppointmentChat.join`; tests `scheduled-chat.test.cjs`, `session-records.test.cjs`. |

| ADR-039 | Session-long local expression cue (approved 2026-10-26 at the client's request; supersedes only the duration and consent cadence of ADR-009/ADR-025): the Counselor starts one optional cue for an appointment call; the Student consents once for that session. The Student's browser then repeats the existing ~3-second local scan (ten frames, ~2-second gap, about five seconds per update) and relays only the current label + confidence with the same request id until either participant stops or the call ends. `unavailable` clears the displayed cue; no timestamps, history, trends, audit rows, or storage are created. ## Registration correction and Users contract update (2026-10-25)

The completed registration-correction and Users Management work is governed by [Registration Information Corrections and Users Management](../docs/REGISTRATION_CORRECTIONS.md). Exact pending-correction COR evidence remains private and temporary until approval, return, replacement, or its original seven-day upload deadline. Operational deactivation is independent of enrollment and revokes sessions. Superadmin never validates University issuance and gains no clinical or counseling permissions.

## Counselor account operations (ADR-035, approved 2026-10-26)

The user explicitly authorized Superadmin creation/editing of Counselor accounts and selected a Superadmin-entered initial password. This supersedes the developer-only/no-provisioning restriction narrowly for Counselor creation and names/email editing. New accounts are server-fixed COUNSELOR/ACTIVE/enabled, with existing Argon2id hashing and no automatic password delivery. Only active non-deactivated Superadmins may mutate them, with CSRF, fresh locks, safe projections, atomic minimal audit and displayed-timestamp stale-edit protection. Email edits revoke all sessions and concurrent login must recheck identity after its User lock; name-only edits preserve sessions. No password reset, arbitrary role/status editing, Student/other-staff profile editing, clinical access or changes to assignments/scheduling are granted. The owning implemented contract is `docs/REGISTRATION_CORRECTIONS.md`.
