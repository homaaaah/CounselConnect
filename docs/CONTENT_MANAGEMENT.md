# CounselConnect — Content and Configuration Management

## Counselor-owned

Counselors manage guidance-office content directly related to counseling delivery, including their schedules, approved office information, and content they are professionally authorized to publish.

## Superadmin-owned

Superadmin manages non-clinical reference configuration, accounts, audit oversight, campus/program data, and operational templates. Superadmin cannot author Counselor assessments or inspect private counseling content by default.

## Removed scope

The Wellness Resource Library, manual resource publication, and automated discovery are removed under ADR-036. Do not add resource publishing, scraping, category, or attachment workflows.

A future navigation/FAQ-only assistant requires ADR-P13 approval.
